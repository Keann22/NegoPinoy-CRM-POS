📌 UPDATE FOR INVENTORY: notes on orders now reach you (Nov 25)

Sales can now send a note from an order straight to Inventory. When they do, you get a pop-up right away: "A note for you".

When it pops up, tap one:
• Answer: reply to them (example: "Checked, 2 pcs on 3C"). Your answer pops up for them right away.
• Got it: you saw it (for "Just letting you know" notes).
• Done: it's taken care of.
• Later: hides it for 30 minutes, then it comes back.

A note to Inventory goes to all of us (Jas, Al, Tess, Edric, Rey). Whoever answers first closes it for everyone.

Can't do it yet? Tap Answer and say why. Don't just close it.

Open notes stay on your Dashboard (For you) and in Tasks until they're answered. They also show on the order page, and in the Picker when you scan the order.

Need something from sales? Write the note on the order, then under "Who is it for?" pick the sales agent (marked "agent"), or pick a name under "Someone else", or type @name in the note. They get a pop-up.
• "Needs a reply": it stays with them until they answer.
• "Just letting you know": it closes when they tap Got it.
• "Just a note": saved on the order only. Nobody gets a pop-up.

Tasks from the owners also pop up right away now.
