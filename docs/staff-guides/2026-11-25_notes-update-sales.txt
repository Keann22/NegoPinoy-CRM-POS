📌 UPDATE FOR SALES: send notes the warehouse will actually see (Nov 25)

Before, a note on an order told no one. Now you choose who gets it, and they get a pop-up right away.

How:
1. Open the order and go to Activity & Notes (you can also add a note from the Priority List).
2. Write your note.
3. Under "Who is it for?" pick:
   • Inventory: the whole warehouse team
   • Owners
   • Someone else: one person
   • or type @name in the note
   "Just a note" = saved on the order only. Nobody gets a pop-up.
4. Pick "Needs a reply" (stays with them until they answer) or "Just letting you know" (closes when they tap Got it).
5. Tap Post Note.

When they answer, the answer pops up for you right away, on any page. It's also saved in the order's Activity & Notes.

If nobody opens your note in 2 hours, you get a pop-up. Message them on Messenger or call them.

To see your notes: Tasks → Notes I sent (Seen / Answered). Sent the wrong one? Tap Withdraw.

Inventory can send notes to you too. When one pops up: Answer, Got it, or Done. Later hides it for 30 minutes.

You don't have to message them on Messenger too. If you picked who it's for, the pop-up reaches them.
