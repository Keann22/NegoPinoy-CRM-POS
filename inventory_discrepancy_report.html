Conclusion on "Wrong Entries"

Your instinct was completely right. The system is generating dozens of wrong entries. Half the time it ignores when you actually buy stock (as seen in the first tables), and the other half of the time it creates massive lists of "Needed" items only to magically erase them a few days later (as seen in the Ghost Items list).

What does this mean?

The data strongly suggests that your inventory sync is broken for receiving purchases. While a few items randomly work (like the Ladder Foldable on June 9 and the 3in1 Fridge Organizer), the vast majority of items are failing to register that new physical stock has arrived, which keeps pushing them back onto your Procurement List over and over again.
